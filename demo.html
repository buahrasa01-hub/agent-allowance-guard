🛡️ Agent Allowance Guard

A policy engine / sandboxed allowance vault for AI agents, live on NEAR testnet. An owner gives an agent a bounded allowance — spending cap, expiry, recipient allowlist — instead of wallet keys. Every spend is checked on-chain and written to an audit log. Built at NEAR HACKATHON.
